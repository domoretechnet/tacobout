        Which 12 items are we comparing?

        To find out whether your city pays more, we price the same order everywhere: one of each of the 12 items below, before tax and discounts.

        Why these 12? They are long-standing menu items sold at at least 97% of the restaurants we sampled. Keeping the order the same makes prices comparable. A restaurant only gets a total if it sells all 12.
